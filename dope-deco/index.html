07 · Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Call or message us and we'll happily talk trees.

When should I book?

It’s first come, first served. December install dates go fast, so book as early as you can.

Do I have to buy the decorations?

No. Everything is rented for the season. We bring it, style it and take it away, so there's nothing to buy or store.

How is the price worked out?

You get a quote based on your requirements, the scale and how soon you need the design. Choose one of our pre-designed signature setups, or have a completely custom setup designed for you.

Will the install disrupt my business?

No. For shops, restaurants, offices and hotels we install out of hours, so your doors stay open.

Can you match our brand colours?

Yes, we can incorporate your brand colours. Send us your palette and we'll design the whole scheme around it.
