Chapter 3 · The approach

From story to launch

Here's how I take a project from our very first chat to a live website. Scroll down and watch the journey draw itself!

  1. Step 1

    Story

    First, I get to know you: who you are, what makes you different, and what your visitors need to understand first.

  2. Step 2

    Page journey

    Next, I map out how visitors meet your story, from the very first screen to that all-important call to action.

  3. Step 3

    Wireframes

    Simple layouts to get the structure just right before any colour goes on.

  4. Step 4

    Design

    The fun part! Your brand guide comes to life through layout, imagery, type and motion, with real personality.

  5. Step 5

    Build

    I work hand in hand with developers to bring the design to life in Webflow, exactly as it was imagined.

  6. Step 6

    Launch

    Final checks on every screen size, then your site goes live. Time to celebrate!

Chapter 4 · About

Designed to be remembered.

I always start with the story. Who is the client, what makes them different, and what does a visitor need to understand? From there I shape the user journey, using layout, imagery, type and motion to carry the brand's personality from the first screen all the way to the call to action.

Branding sits at the heart of everything I do. I take each client's brand guide, from logo and colours to tone of voice, and use it to craft a storytelling experience that feels truly theirs, never generic.
